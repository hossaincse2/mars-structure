Home FAQ
Contact our sales team via phone, WhatsApp, or by visiting our sales office. Our advisors will guide you through project selection, unit availability, and help complete the booking form. A booking money amount (non-refundable) will be collected to formally reserve your preferred unit.
You can initiate your booking inquiry online through our Contact page. However, formal documentation and payment must be completed in person or through our authorized representatives for legal compliance and security.
We accept bank transfers, pay orders, demand drafts, and cheques payable to Mars Structure Limited. We also facilitate tripartite loan agreements with leading banks. Cash payments above BDT 5 lakh are not accepted per Bangladesh Bank regulations.
Yes, we work with multiple nationalized and private banks for tripartite home loan financing. Our advisors assist in document preparation and loan processing. Typical financing covers 70-80% of property value based on eligibility.
Schedules are tied to construction milestones: Booking Money → Down Payment (upon Sale Agreement) → 6-12 quarterly installments linked to construction progress → Final payment before possession. The exact schedule is confirmed in your Sale Agreement.
Our flagship project — Nur-Anu Oasis — is in Jolshiri Abashon Smart City, Purbachal, Dhaka. We also develop properties in Gulshan, Banani, and Baridhara. Visit our Property Locations page for a detailed map.
Yes, during the pre-construction phase, buyers can request customizations including flooring, fixtures, wall finishes, kitchen cabinets, and bathroom fittings. Structural modifications are subject to architectural approval. Our concierge team coordinates all custom requests.
Amenities include: rooftop garden & sky lounge, infinity swimming pool, fully equipped gymnasium, business center, prayer hall, multi-cuisine restaurant, children's playland, 24/7 CCTV & security, covered parking, and generator backup. Availability varies by project.
The estimated handover date is specified in your Sale Agreement. We provide regular construction updates and scheduled site visits. While we strive to meet all timelines, force majeure events may cause delays, which we communicate transparently to all buyers.
You will receive: Registered Sale Deed, Possession Certificate, Occupation Certificate, Utility connection documents, As-Built floor plans, Warranty cards for all appliances and fixtures, and a maintenance handbook.
Yes, all Mars Structure Limited projects have proper RAJUK building plan approvals, Jolshiri Abashon Authority clearances, environmental clearances, and fire safety certifications. All approval documents can be provided upon request during due diligence.
Transfer to a third party is possible subject to written approval from Mars Structure Limited and a nominal transfer fee. Both parties must be present for documentation. Transfer is not permitted during the initial 6-month lock-in period.
