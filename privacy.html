Your Privacy Matters

Mars Structure Limited respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personally identifiable information you share with us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website, services, and personnel.

1

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

Personal Identity
  • • Full name, NID/Passport number
  • • Date of birth, nationality
  • • Photographs (KYC requirements)
Contact Details
  • • Email address, phone number
  • • Residential / mailing address
  • • Emergency contact information
Financial Details
  • • Bank account / TIN information
  • • Payment transaction records
  • • Loan / financing documentation
Digital Data
  • • IP address, browser type
  • • Pages visited, time spent
  • • Cookie identifiers
2

How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Processing property bookings, sale agreements, and payment schedules.
  • Communicating property updates, construction milestones, and handover schedules.
  • Conducting KYC (Know Your Customer) verification as required by Bangladesh Bank Anti-Money Laundering guidelines.
  • Sending newsletters, property listings, and promotional content (with your consent).
  • Improving our website user experience through analytics and performance monitoring.
  • Legal compliance, dispute resolution, and enforcement of our agreements.
3

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Banks & Financial Institutions: For processing home loans and financing arrangements (only with your consent).
  • Legal & Regulatory Authorities: When required by law, court order, or government regulation.
  • Trusted Service Providers: Including lawyers, architects, notary services, and registration offices for property documentation.
  • Analytics Partners: Aggregated, anonymized data only, for improving our digital services.
4

Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Types of cookies we use:

Essential
Required for site functionality
Analytics
Track visits & page performance
Marketing
Relevant property ads & offers

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

5

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all online data transmissions.
  • Secure servers with restricted access and regular security audits.
  • Staff training on data protection and confidentiality obligations.
  • Access controls ensuring only authorized personnel can access sensitive data.

Note: No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6

Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by Bangladesh tax and financial regulations. Digital browsing data is retained for a maximum of 24 months.

7

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access
Request a copy of data we hold
Rectification
Correct inaccurate personal data
Erasure
Request deletion (subject to legal retention)
Opt-Out
Unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@marsproperties.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Google Maps, YouTube, social media). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

9

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately so we can delete it promptly.
